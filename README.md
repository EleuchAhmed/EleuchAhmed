## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Computer Science engineering student at **INSA Lyon** (previously at INSAT, Tunis) focused on **securing applications, cloud infrastructure, networks and CI/CD pipelines**. I like understanding how systems break, then building the detections and defenses that stop it: from rogue IPv6 router advertisements to ARP spoofing to full MITRE ATT&CK attack chains.

I also build **agentic AI** tooling to automate research and learning, and I enjoy shipping end-to-end software.

## 💼 Experience

- 🏢 **IS Division Member, ETIC INSA Technologies** (Oct 2026 - present): client projects in software engineering, automation and secure system design at INSA Lyon's Junior-Entreprise.
- - 🛡️ **Cybersecurity Intern, EY** (Jul - Aug 2026): built an isolated purple team lab (Splunk SIEM, Windows and Linux targets, Kali), simulated attack chains and wrote detection rules mapped to **MITRE ATT&CK**, then validated them by replaying the full attack.
  - - 🏦 **Software Developer Intern, BNA** (Jun - Jul 2026): worked on modernizing a critical banking information system through process automation and AI integration, under strict security and compliance requirements.
    - - 📣 **Media and Communication Manager, IEEE INSAT SB** (Feb 2025 - Jul 2026): Executive Board member of the largest IEEE Student Branch in Tunisia.
